Derive the Data Jargon

How to play

  1. Study the rebus — the layout is the clue, not decoration.
  2. Say what you see out loud; these puzzles reward the ear.
  3. Type your guess.
  4. Solve the set to finish the round.

What it teaches

Data has a vocabulary problem: words like median, outlier, schema and aggregate sound forbidding long before they are difficult. Meeting them as puzzles rather than definitions removes the intimidation — a word you have solved is a word you own. By the end a player has handled a working data vocabulary without ever having sat through a glossary.
