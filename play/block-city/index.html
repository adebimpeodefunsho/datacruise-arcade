Bug-Bug's Block City

How to play

  1. Read the number each bar needs to reach.
  2. Stack blocks to grow that bar.
  3. Stop at exactly the right height — overshooting counts as a miss.
  4. Build every bar in the row to finish the city.

What it teaches

A bar chart compares separate things side by side: four flavours, six countries, three months of sales. What makes it work is that every bar starts from the same baseline, so height alone carries the comparison. Stacking the bars by hand makes that obvious in a way looking at a finished chart does not — a bar is tall because it holds more blocks, and nothing else. That is also the quiet lesson behind why a bar chart with a chopped-off axis misleads people.
