Bridge the Data Sentence

How to play

  1. Read the broken sentence from both ends.
  2. Pick the words that bridge the middle.
  3. Check it still means something true once it is whole.
  4. Complete five sentences to clear the round.

What it teaches

Knowing a word is not the same as being able to use it. These sentences put terms back into working order — the median is the middle value when the numbers are sorted — so the vocabulary arrives already attached to its grammar. It is also quiet practice at saying a finding out loud, which is the step most people find hardest once they have actually done the analysis.
