Crack the Data Crossword

How to play

  1. Pick a clue from the across or down list.
  2. Type the answer into the grid.
  3. Use the letters you have already placed to crack the harder clues.
  4. Fill the grid before the timer runs out.

What it teaches

A crossword forces recall rather than recognition — it is one thing to nod at the word variance in a sentence and quite another to produce it from a definition and seven letters. The crossing letters do something useful too: they let a half-remembered word be reconstructed from partial evidence, which is a surprisingly honest rehearsal of how real analysis often feels.
