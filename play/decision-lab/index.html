Bug-Bug's Decision Lab

How to play

  1. Look at the chart on the bench.
  2. Read the question underneath it.
  3. Pick the answer the chart actually supports.
  4. Pass all five rounds to complete the lab.

What it teaches

This is the game that checks whether the other ten landed. Reading a chart well means more than naming the tallest bar: it means noticing what the axis starts at, telling a real difference from a tiny one, and resisting a conclusion the data does not support. The questions are built to reward looking twice, so a confident wrong answer is corrected by the chart itself rather than by being told off.
