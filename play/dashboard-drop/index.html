How to play

  1. Catch a falling chart before it lands.
  2. Drop it into the panel where it belongs.
  3. Think about what sits next to what — order carries meaning.
  4. Fill the board to finish the dashboard.

What it teaches

A dashboard is not a pile of charts; it is an argument made of charts. The big number goes where the eye lands first, the trend sits near the thing it explains, and related charts sit together so they can be compared without hunting. Arranging the panels by hand introduces layout as a decision rather than an accident — which is the single biggest difference between a dashboard people use and one they ignore.
